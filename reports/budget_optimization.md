# Budget Optimization Findings

## Actionable Insights for Media Investment Decisions

### 1. Reallocate Budget to High-ROI Seasonal and Furniture Campaigns

**Finding**: Seasonal and Furniture product lines tie for the highest ROI at 2.72x, yet receive only 20% and 19% of total spend respectively. Appliances receives 40% of spend but delivers lower ROI at 2.61x.

**Recommendation**: Shift 10-15% of Appliances budget to Seasonal and Furniture campaigns.

**Expected Impact**: Potential improvement of 0.11x blended ROI, translating to ~$300K additional conversion value.


### 2. Campaign 1214 Is Underfunded Despite Strong Performance

**Finding**: `HomeBase_Seasonal_National` (1214) has a CPA of $28.12, significantly below the $45.67 average, but received only $37,814 in spend.

**Recommendation**: Increase budget for Campaign 1214. If performance holds, doubling spend would yield ~2,700 additional conversions at below-average cost.

**Expected Impact**: ~$17 savings per acquisition compared to average.


### 3. Campaign 1090 Requires Immediate Budget Review

**Finding**: HomeBase_Appliances_National (1090) spent \$427,400 against a \$36,958 budget, 1156% of budget. ROI is 2.61x (below category average).

**Recommendation**: Investigate whether overspend is intentional (scaling a successful campaign) or a budget-setting error. If intentional, update budget records. If unintentional, review campaign controls.


### 4. Google Delivers Comparable ROI with Less Spend

**Finding**: Google ROI (3.3x) is comparable to Meta (3.0x) but Google receives only 32% of total spend.

**Recommendation**: Test increasing Google budget by 10-15%. If ROI holds, blended ROI could improve.


### 5. Optimize Store Visit Timing and Regional Allocation

**Finding**: Cost per store visit varies significantly:
- **East region**: $2.34 avg — most efficient (55% cheaper than National)
- **West region**: $3.09 avg — efficient
- **National**: $5.22 avg — least efficient, but covers all 15 DMAs
- Best: West week 2 at $0.38 vs Worst: National week 13 at $11.00

**Recommendation**: 
1. Investigate why East regional campaigns achieve 55% lower cost per visit
2. Consider extending East/West regional campaigns beyond week 16
3. Analyze National efficiency — are costs diluted across too many DMAs?

**Expected Impact**: If 10% of National spend ($444K) achieved East-level efficiency ($2.34 vs $5.22), savings of ~$128K in customer acquisition costs.
